The Sacred Course

Challenge Overview

◆
Part 1

Balloon Vanquisher

The Self-Balancing Robot — bearing a needle — ventures forth to recognise AprilTags, align with precision, and burst a balloon before halting within the sacred Stop Zone.

Part 2

The Villain's Reckoning

The Engineer Robot grasps a red orb, advances to the Stop Line, recognises the villain's visage through facial recognition, and launches the orb — striking only the villain while sparing the innocent.

Part 3

Ascent of the Sage

Guided by pose recognition, the Engineer Robot ascends a 30° slope, weaves through mystical cone obstacles, and claims the Stop Zone atop the stage.

Part 4

The Great Sorting

Bearing a bulldozer blade, the Engineer Robot autonomously scours the arena — recognising red and blue blocks by colour, classifying them, and pushing each to its ordained zone.
